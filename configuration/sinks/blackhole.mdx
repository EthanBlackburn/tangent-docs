---
title: "Blackhole"
description: "Discard records."
---

The **Blackhole sink** discards everything it gets. Use it for benchmarking, or to end a DAG whose plugin's effect is elsewhere (for example, in state).

---

## Schema

<ParamField path="type" type="string" required>
Must be `blackhole`.
</ParamField>

---

## Example

```yaml tangent.yaml icon=upload
sinks:
  blackhole:
    type: blackhole
```
